## Version 1: For a 5-Year-Old

### What is Scale AMM?
A magical toy store where new toys become more expensive as more kids want to buy them.

### What problem does it solve?
**The Problem**: Imagine you invented a cool new toy and want to sell it, but you don't have money to open a store.

**The Solution**: Scale AMM lets you open the store for FREE! You just bring your toys, and the store figures out the prices by itself.

### How does it work?
**The Lemonade Stand Story**:

1. You have 100 stickers you made
2. You put them in a magic box that sells them for you
3. First sticker costs 1 penny
4. As kids buy stickers, each one costs a little more (2 pennies, then 3, then 4...)
5. When you've collected 50 pennies total, the magic box becomes a real store that stays open forever!

**The magic**: A "price helper" watches how much special money (called CRX) is worth and keeps everything fair.

### What are the key parts?

1. **The Magic Box** (Pool)
   - Holds your toys and does all the math

2. **The Price Helper** (Oracle)
   - Makes sure prices are fair by checking how much real money everything is worth

3. **Special Money** (CRX tokens)
   - The coins everyone uses to buy toys in this store

4. **Your Toys** (New tokens)
   - The thing you invented that you want to sell

5. **The Growing Phase** (PreBonding)
   - When prices go up as people buy

6. **The Forever Store** (Graduated)
   - When you've collected enough special money, the store stays open forever

7. **Bot Blocker** (Anti-sniper)
   - Stops mean robots from buying everything at once and ruining the fun

### What happens when you use it?

**Story: Timmy Launches His Sticker Collection**

1. **Monday**: Timmy creates 1,000 superhero stickers
2. **Monday afternoon**: He puts them in the magic box and says "I want each sticker to be worth 1 penny to start"
3. **Tuesday**: His friend Emma buys 10 stickers for 10 pennies total
4. **Tuesday later**: More friends hear about it! Now stickers cost 2 pennies each because they're popular
5. **Wednesday**: 50 kids have bought stickers, and Timmy's box has collected 500 pennies
6. **MAGIC TIME**: The box says "You reached your goal!" and becomes a permanent store
7. **Thursday and forever**: The store keeps running! Price goes up when people buy, down when people sell, but the store NEVER closes

### Why is it better?

**Old Way** (Like a normal store):
- Need $100 to open the store
- Have to sit there all day selling
- Store might close if you run out of money
- Hard for new kids to start

**New Way** (Scale AMM):
- FREE to start! No money needed
- Store runs by itself (magic!)
- Store NEVER closes once it's permanent
- Anyone can open a store

**The Best Part**:
- Stops bullies (bots) from buying everything immediately
- Prices are always fair because the "price helper" watches them

## Version 2: For a Non-Technical Adult

### What is Scale AMM?
A self-running marketplace where anyone can launch new digital assets without upfront capital, with prices that automatically adjust based on supply and demand.

### What problem does it solve?

**Real-World Problem**: Imagine you're a creator with a new digital collectible (like trading cards, art tokens, or community points) you want to launch. Traditionally, you'd need:
- Thousands of dollars upfront to create liquidity
- Complex technical knowledge
- Someone to constantly manage the market
- Risk of the market dying if you can't sustain it

**The Solution**: Scale AMM is like Etsy meets the stock market. You deposit your items once, and the system:
- Creates a market automatically (no money needed)
- Adjusts prices as people buy and sell
- Eventually becomes a permanent marketplace
- Runs itself completely

### How does it work?

**The Farmer's Market Analogy**:

Think of it like a smart vending machine at a farmer's market:

1. **You bring your products**: You show up with 10,000 jars of homemade jam
2. **The machine figures out pricing**: It looks at similar products and says "Let's start at $1 per jar"
3. **Prices adjust automatically**:
   - First 100 jars sell at $1-$2 each
   - As demand grows, prices rise to $3, $4, $5...
   - If people sell jars back, prices go down
4. **The machine collects payment**: As jars sell, it accumulates cash in a locked box
5. **Graduation day**: When the box has $5,000 saved up, the vending machine transforms into a permanent 24/7 store
6. **Forever market**: Your jam can be bought and sold forever, even while you sleep

**The Smart Part**: A "price checker" (think: someone who monitors real-world prices) ensures everything stays pegged to actual dollar values, even if the payment method (special tokens called CRX) fluctuates in value.

### What are the key parts?

1. **The Pool** (Your Marketplace)
   - Container holding your digital assets
   - Automatically calculates prices
   - Handles all transactions

2. **Price Oracle** (The Fair Price Monitor)
   - Checks real-world dollar values every minute
   - Adjusts calculations so $10 is always $10
   - Prevents price manipulation

3. **CRX Tokens** (The Universal Currency)
   - Digital money used for all transactions
   - Like having one currency for all farmer's markets globally
   - Gains value as more markets use it

4. **Your Tokens** (The Product)
   - Your digital creation (art, collectibles, points, etc.)
   - Fixed supply - can't create more
   - Tradeable like baseball cards

5. **PreBonding Phase** (The Growth Stage)
   - First days/weeks when you're building momentum
   - Prices rise with demand
   - Anti-bot protection active
   - "Virtual" liquidity (calculated, not real yet)

6. **Graduated Phase** (The Permanent Market)
   - Once you hit your goal, becomes permanent
   - Real money locked in forever
   - Works like a traditional stock exchange
   - Can never be shut down

7. **Anti-Sniper System** (Bot Protection)
   - First 60 seconds: limits how much anyone can buy
   - Penalties for quick flippers
   - Protects real humans from bot attacks

### What happens when you use it?

**Real Example: Sarah Launches Her Digital Art Collection**

**Day 1 - Creation**:
- Sarah creates 50,000 digital art tokens
- She wants to launch at a $10,000 market value
- She chooses a $40,000 graduation target
- Cost to her: $0 (just pays a small transaction fee)

**Day 2-7 - Early Trading**:
- Art collectors discover her work
- First buyers get art for $0.20 each (10,000 market cap ÷ 50,000 tokens)
- As 10,000 tokens sell, the price rises to $0.80 each
- Bot protection prevents whales from buying everything

**Day 8-20 - Building Momentum**:
- Word spreads, more buyers arrive
- Price climbs to $1.50 per token
- Sarah's pool has accumulated $25,000 in CRX tokens
- She's earning 1% fees on every trade (her choice)

**Day 21 - GRADUATION**:
- Pool hits $40,000 in accumulated CRX
- System automatically "graduates" the pool
- All that CRX is now locked permanently
- The marketplace is now eternal

**Day 22 and Forever**:
- Anyone can buy/sell Sarah's art 24/7/365
- Prices still fluctuate with supply/demand
- Sarah still earns fees
- The market can NEVER be shut down
- Works exactly like trading stocks

### Why is it better?

**Traditional Way** (Like Uniswap or Raydium):
- Need $10,000+ upfront to create liquidity
- Risk losing your money if market fails
- Constant maintenance required
- Bots can snipe your launch

**Scale AMM Way**:
- $0 upfront (truly free to launch)
- No risk of losing capital
- 100% automated
- Bot protection built-in
- Oracle ensures stable dollar pricing
- Marketplace becomes permanent

**Real-World Wins**:

1. **For Creators**: Launch cost drops from $10,000 to $0
2. **For Buyers**: Fair launches, no bot domination
3. **For the Ecosystem**: CRX becomes deflationary (locked in graduated pools forever)
4. **For Everyone**: Permanent markets mean your assets have value forever

**The Secret Sauce**:
Virtual liquidity (calculated prices) transitions to real liquidity (actual money) automatically. It's like starting with an IOU that becomes real cash when you hit your goal.

## Version 3: For a Developer (But Still Simple)

### What is Scale AMM?
An automated market maker protocol with oracle-integrated virtual liquidity that transitions to constant-product AMM after reaching TVL thresholds - think Pump.fun meets Uniswap with dynamic USD-pegged reserves.

### What problem does it solve?

**Technical Problem**: Bootstrapping liquidity for new tokens is capital-intensive and risky:
- Uniswap v2/v3 requires depositing equal-value assets (e.g., $10k of Token A + $10k of USDC)
- Early LPs risk impermanent loss
- Failed launches leave capital stranded
- No mechanism to reach "escape velocity" and graduate to sustainable liquidity

**Scale AMM's Solution**: Virtual reserves + gradual accumulation + automatic graduation
- Launch with 0 capital (oracle calculates virtual reserves from USD target)
- Real reserves accumulate from trading fees
- Auto-graduates to permanent x×y=k AMM when threshold met
- Capital efficiency: launch 1000 tokens with the capital previously needed for 1

### How does it work?

**Technical Flow**:

```
Phase 1: PreBonding (Virtual Liquidity)
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Target: $10k market cap             │
│ CRX price: $2 (from Pyth oracle)    │
│ Supply: 100,000 tokens              │
│                                     │
│ Calculation:                        │
│ virtual_reserves = (10,000 / 2)     │
│                  = 5,000 CRX        │
│                                     │
│ Price: 5,000 / 100,000 = 0.05 CRX   │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘

Trades happen:
- Users buy tokens with CRX
- CRX accumulates in vault (real_quote_reserves grows)
- Pricing still uses virtual_reserves (not real)
- Anti-sniper active (size limits + time decay penalties)

Phase 2: Graduation Trigger
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ When real_quote_reserves ≥          │
│      graduation_threshold_usd       │
│                                     │
│ Execute graduation:                 │
│ 1. Freeze virtual reserves          │
│ 2. Switch to real reserves          │
│ 3. Lock all CRX permanently         │
│ 4. Disable anti-sniper              │
│ 5. Formula: x × y = k               │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘

Phase 3: Graduated (Real Liquidity)
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Constant Product AMM                │
│ x = real_base_reserves (tokens)     │
│ y = real_quote_reserves (CRX)       │
│ k = x × y (constant)                │
│                                     │
│ Standard Uniswap v2 math:           │
│ Δy = (y × Δx) / (x + Δx)            │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### What are the key parts?

**1. Pool Account** (PDA)
```rust
pub struct Pool {
    pub base_mint: Pubkey,           // Token being launched
    pub quote_mint: Pubkey,          // CRX (or whitelisted)
    pub base_vault: Pubkey,          // SPL token account (holds tokens)
    pub quote_vault: Pubkey,         // SPL token account (holds CRX)

    // Phase control
    pub phase: PoolPhase,            // PreBonding | Graduated
    pub graduation_threshold_usd: u64,

    // Virtual reserves (PreBonding)
    pub virtual_base_reserves: u64,
    pub virtual_quote_reserves: u64,

    // Real reserves (Graduated)
    pub real_base_reserves: u64,
    pub real_quote_reserves: u64,

    // Configuration
    pub bonding_curve_type: BondingCurveType,
    pub creator_fee_bps: u16,
    pub anti_sniper_config: AntiSniperConfig,
}
```

**2. Oracle Module** (Pyth Integration)
- Fetches CRX/USD price feed
- Validates: freshness (<60s), confidence (<1%), exponent bounds (-12 to +6)
- Used for: virtual reserve calculation, USD-to-CRX conversions
- Prevents: stale price manipulation, invalid data attacks

**3. Bonding Curve Math**
```rust
// ConstantProduct (PreBonding)
Δy = (y × Δx) / (x + Δx)

// Exponential (PreBonding)
Δy = (x × Y × Δx) / ((X + 1.5×Δx) × (x + Δx))

// Graduated (always ConstantProduct)
x × y = k
```

**4. Anti-Sniper System**
```rust
pub struct AntiSniperConfig {
    pub max_trade_percentage: u16,   // e.g., 500 = 5%
    pub time_window_slots: u64,      // e.g., 100 slots ≈ 40s
    pub penalty_bps: u16,            // e.g., 1000 = 10% max penalty
}

// Penalty calculation (time-decaying)
let time_factor = slots_since_creation / time_window_slots;
let penalty = base_penalty_bps * (1 - time_factor);  // Saturating math
```

**5. Fee Distribution**
```rust
Protocol Fee (1%):    → Config.fee_recipient
Creator Fee (0-1%):   → Pool.creator_wallet
WAA Penalty (0-10%):  → Config.fee_recipient (burn/redistribution)
```

**6. Graduation Mechanism**
```rust
fn check_graduation(pool: &Pool) -> bool {
    // Convert USD threshold to CRX using oracle
    let threshold_crx = oracle.usd_to_crx(pool.graduation_threshold_usd);

    // Check if accumulated real reserves meet threshold
    if pool.real_quote_reserves >= threshold_crx {
        // Trigger graduation
        pool.phase = PoolPhase::Graduated;
        pool.virtual_base_reserves = pool.real_base_reserves;
        pool.virtual_quote_reserves = pool.real_quote_reserves;
        // CRX now locked forever in quote_vault
        return true;
    }
    false
}
```

### Why is it better?

**Comparison Table**:

| Feature | Uniswap v2 | Pump.fun | Scale AMM |
|---------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Launch capital | $10k-$100k | $0 | $0 |
| Liquidity type | Real | Virtual (permanent) | Virtual → Real |
| Can graduate? | N/A | No | Yes (auto) |
| Anti-sniper | No | Yes (time-based) | Yes (time + size) |
| Oracle integration | No | No | Yes (Pyth) |
| USD-stable pricing | No | No | Yes |
| Permanent AMM | Yes | No | Yes (post-grad) |
| Capital efficiency | 1x | ∞ (but temp) | ∞ → 1x (permanent) |

**Key Innovations**:

1. **Zero-capital bootstrapping**: Virtual reserves eliminate upfront cost
2. **Graduation mechanism**: Transition from virtual to real creates permanent liquidity
3. **Oracle-stabilized**: USD-denominated targets stay accurate despite CRX volatility
4. **Anti-sniper built-in**: Time decay + size limits prevent bot exploitation
5. **Deflationary sink**: Graduated pools lock CRX forever (can't withdraw)

**Attack Vectors Mitigated**:

- Overflow attacks: All arithmetic uses `checked_*` operations
- Oracle manipulation: Staleness + confidence + bounds validation
- Sandwich attacks: Slippage protection on every trade
- Flash loan attacks: Reentrancy guards + vault balance validation
- Sniping: WAA system + size limits during launch window

**Gas Optimization**:
- Current: ~100k compute units per trade (target: <50k)
- Uses PDAs (no rent-exempt deposits)
- Events for off-chain indexing (reduces on-chain data)
